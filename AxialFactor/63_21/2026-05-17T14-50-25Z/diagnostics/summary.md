## Architectural implications

**Multiple issues** (probe 1.5 localises the root cause; the rest are mostly downstream consequences):

  - **Collapse at the query level** (probe 1.5): factor_queries has effective rank 1.10, all pairwise cos-sim near +1. Family 1 fix (orthogonal init or orthogonality penalty on factor_queries) is the right starting point.

  - Factor tokens carry no regime information (probe 1): between/within variance ratios ≈ 0.054, regime classifier 27.1% vs 25.2% baseline.

  - Regime-blind predicted trajectories (probe 3) are a *downstream consequence* of the extractor collapse — the temporal map is being fed regime-blind tokens, so its output cannot vary by regime.

  - Cross-factor top-1 horizon trajectories nearly identical (probe 2: max |off-diag| 0.999). The four factors share one horizon shape, up to sign.

  - Individual spatial loadings are well-learned (probe 4: smooth, rank-1-separable, high cosine to top training PC) — the static-reconstruction *prior* is doing its job; it's the factor decomposition feeding it that has collapsed.
