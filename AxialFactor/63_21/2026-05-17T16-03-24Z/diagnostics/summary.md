## Architectural implications

**Multiple issues** (probe 1.5 localises the root cause; the rest are mostly downstream consequences):

  - Parameter-level SVD (probe 1.5) does not show a clean rank-1 collapse: queries eff. rank 3.97, loadings eff. rank 1.72. Any near-collinearity downstream emerges from the attention + temporal pipeline rather than being baked into the parameters.

  - Temporal map is regime-blind even though the extractor isn't (probe 3): predicted-g spread across regimes is much smaller than true-g spread.

  - Cross-factor top-1 horizon trajectories nearly identical (probe 2: max |off-diag| 0.998). The four factors share one horizon shape, up to sign.

  - Individual spatial loadings are well-learned (probe 4: smooth, rank-1-separable, high cosine to top training PC) — the static-reconstruction *prior* is doing its job; it's the factor decomposition feeding it that has collapsed.
